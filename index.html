The mandate behind the title

Growth has made coordination consequential.

Metrc's operating environment now combines government contracts, market transitions, platform modernization, expanding product lines, integrator relationships, and a broad user ecosystem. The Chief of Staff is the mechanism that keeps those priorities connected to executive decisions and measurable outcomes - without absorbing functional ownership.

The company moment is not simply "more growth." It is more simultaneous change.

Metrc reported 30 regulatory contracts and more than 500,000 users in January 2026, while highlighting a refreshed UI, a state-configured Industry Sandbox, and a shift toward strategic product management. Retail ID had already reached a one-million-weekly QR-code run rate across 21 markets, and a BioTrack partnership created another important operating interface.

Chief of Staff hypothesis: the CEO needs one clear trace of strategic intent, ownership, dependency, decision, evidence, and closure across this expanding portfolio.

Public signals: Metrc official company, leadership, product, and 2025-2026 newsroom pages. See source notes in the interview brief.

The operating model

The Strategic Trace Record

Every CEO-level priority receives a lightweight, visible record. Not a status deck. Not a parallel project-management bureaucracy. A decision-quality trace that makes ownership, constraints, and next actions inspectable.

Open the Priority Trace Review
01

Outcome tag

Define the business or public-value outcome in a way that can be recognized when achieved.

02

Accountable custody

Name the executive owner, operating owner, and the work that remains with each function.

03

Dependency map

Expose the minimum cross-functional, market, partner, data, and customer conditions required for movement.

04

Decision threshold

State what can continue locally, what needs executive judgment, and what triggers escalation or stop.

05

Evidence receipt

Connect progress to a small number of outcome, adoption, reliability, trust, and economic measures.

06

Closure or learning

Record the decision, result, unresolved risk, and reusable learning so the organization compounds rather than repeats.

Executive operating rhythm

Cadence should create decisions, not meeting gravity.

The trace record feeds a small set of executive rhythms. Each rhythm has a different job, decision horizon, and evidence standard.

Weekly

Priority exception review

Resolve cross-functional constraints and attention shifts.

  • Only exceptions and decisions
  • Owner and due date explicit
  • Closed work leaves the agenda
Monthly

Business trace review

Connect strategic initiatives to operating and customer evidence.

  • Outcome and adoption signals
  • Portfolio tradeoffs
  • Stop / sustain / scale calls
Quarterly

Strategy-to-capacity reset

Reconcile ambition, capacity, economics, and risk.

  • Resource concentration
  • Cross-market learning
  • Priority retirement
Event-driven

Decision huddle

Convene only when a threshold is crossed.

  • Pre-read from trace record
  • Named decision maker
  • Decision receipt in 24 hours

First 120 days

Earn the right to simplify.

The first move is not to install a new meeting architecture. It is to understand where Metrc's existing rhythms already work, where decision latency accumulates, and which strategic priorities require a clearer trace.

DAYS 0-30

Read the operating system

Listen across the CEO, ELT, board-facing priorities, major initiatives, customer and government commitments, and existing reviews.

Deliverable: operating-rhythm and decision-latency map
DAYS 31-60

Pilot the trace

Select two high-value priorities and build lightweight trace records using existing data, meetings, and owners.

Deliverable: two live priority traces and decision thresholds
DAYS 61-90

Tighten the cadence

Remove duplicate reporting, clarify executive forums, and create an exception-based follow-through discipline.

Deliverable: CEO / ELT cadence charter and portfolio view
DAYS 91-120

Transfer and compound

Institutionalize ownership, document learning, and expand only where the mechanism improves decisions and outcomes.

Deliverable: scalable operating playbook and learning registry

Executive discovery

Questions that reveal the real job.

These are designed to test the hypothesis, not to assume internal conditions that are not publicly known.

Which two or three priorities most need the CEO's attention to move - and which currently consume attention without changing outcomes?Separates strategic leverage from executive noise.
Where does Metrc most often lose decision velocity: incomplete information, unclear ownership, cross-functional dependencies, or risk tolerance?Locates the operating constraint before prescribing cadence.
How should the Chief of Staff divide work with the executive team so the role increases accountability rather than centralizing it?Clarifies decision rights and protects functional ownership.
What would make platform modernization, market implementations, and product expansion feel like one coherent company strategy rather than parallel programs?Tests the need for a shared strategic trace.
At the end of year one, what evidence would convince the CEO and board that this role materially improved execution?Establishes outcome measures before activity measures.
