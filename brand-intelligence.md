## Typography evidence and decision
The current Metrc website exhibits a rounded, modern geometric sans-serif character with strong, concise headlines and generous spacing. No publicly licensed Metrc font package was identified during research. The campaign uses the documented substitute stack `Avenir Next`, `Segoe UI`, `Helvetica Neue`, Arial, sans-serif. No proprietary font files are copied or committed.

## Photography, product, interface, and geometry cues
- Secure, practical supply-chain and facility imagery
- RFID tags, scanning, chain-of-custody, dashboards, data visibility, and state-market context
- Rounded geometry, large concise headlines, calm whitespace, forest-green recognition layer
- Product tone: trustworthy, technical, clear, regulated, modern, and accessible

## Role-derived visual translation
The campaign does not copy Metrc product UI. It translates the company's traceability logic into an original executive operating artifact: the Executive Priority Trace. A strategic priority receives an outcome tag, accountable custody, dependency map, decision threshold, evidence receipt, and closure record.

## Motion character
A subtle scan moves across the five visible trace stages in the hero. Scenario selection changes the strategic intent, risk, ownership, dependencies, decision, outcome, and executive state. Reduced-motion mode disables travel animation while preserving the complete system and every scenario.

## Independent-candidate distinction
Every surface states that the campaign is an independent candidate work product and that no Metrc affiliation or endorsement is implied. The official mark is used only to identify the target employer.
