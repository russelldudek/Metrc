Executive Priority Trace
A lightweight review mechanism for CEO-level priorities
Independent candidate artifact
Russell Dudek
https://russelldudek.github.io/Metrc/
Illustrative baseline - not internal Metrc data

Purpose

Make one strategic priority decision-ready without replacing the functional plan. Complete only the fields required to clarify outcome, ownership, dependencies, thresholds, evidence, and closure.

Illustrative trace: platform modernization

Outcome tag
Modernize the platform without destabilizing daily regulatory work.
Desired result: improved reliability, performance, usability, and strategic product capacity with controlled user adoption.
Accountable custody
Executive owner: CTO with CEO sponsorship.
Operating owners: product, engineering, release, customer experience, support, and market-facing leaders retain functional authority.
Dependencies
User research, regulator and operator feedback, reliability evidence, migration posture, training, support readiness, integrator impacts, and market-specific constraints.
Decision threshold
Next decision: Which release scope and adoption path meet the minimum readiness threshold?
Escalate when: reliability, continuity, or cross-market effects exceed functional authority or accepted risk.
Evidence receipt
Reliability and performance trends; user task completion and friction; support demand; migration and adoption; integrator readiness; unresolved exceptions; business value.
Closure / learning
Record the decision, release posture, owner commitments, residual risks, user response, and reusable pattern for the next release or market.

Review questions

QuestionDecision-useful answer
What changed since the last review?One material fact, risk, dependency, or assumption - not a status narrative.
Why does it matter now?Effect on outcome, trust, timing, economics, adoption, or regulatory / customer commitments.
Who owns resolution?Named executive and operating owner; functional boundaries remain explicit.
What decision is required?A specific choice, approval, tradeoff, escalation, or no-action call.
What evidence is sufficient?Minimum information needed to decide; avoid analysis without a decision horizon.
What happens next?Owner, due date, communication, and whether the trace remains open or closes.

Balanced evidence frame

OutcomeIs the intended result moving?
ReliabilityIs the system or service stable?
AdoptionAre users changing behavior?
EconomicsIs value or capacity improving?
TrustAre stakeholders confident?
LearningWhat should be reused or stopped?

Closure receipt

Decision
What was decided, by whom, and when?
Commitments
Which owners accepted which actions and timing?
Communication
Who needs to understand the decision, rationale, and next state?
Residual risk
What remains uncertain or intentionally accepted?
Learning
What pattern should influence the next market, release, product, or partnership decision?
