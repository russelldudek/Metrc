Why the role matters now

Metrc is scaling the company behind the system of record.

The operating challenge is not simply more growth. It is keeping one executive truth while platform, markets, products, partners, regulators, and users move at different speeds.

01

Platform modernization

Metrc has publicly described a shift toward enterprise-grade product development, a refreshed interface, and strategic product management.

02

Thirty contracted markets

A platform serving more than 500,000 users must coordinate national consistency with real market-specific requirements.

03

Broader ecosystem moves

Partnerships, APIs, Retail ID, inspection tools, and user exchanges increase strategic surface area - and the need for explicit ownership.

04

Trust is the product

Reliability, transparency, regulatory integrity, and user confidence cannot become collateral damage of faster innovation.

What changes in the room

The cadence moves from updates to decisions.

Without a trace

×Status is assembled during the meeting.
×Ownership shifts between functions and follow-up threads.
×Every issue competes for executive escalation.
×Closure means activity completed, not outcome verified.

With Executive Priority Trace

✓Pre-read isolates exceptions, decisions, and changed assumptions.
✓One accountable owner retains custody through closure.
✓Thresholds distinguish local variance from executive risk.
✓Evidence closes the loop and updates the next decision.

First 120 days

Learn the system. Instrument the priorities. Prove the cadence. Scale what works.

Days 1-30

Trace the work

Build trust before changing the rhythm.

  • CEO and ELT mandate interviews
  • Priority and meeting inventory
  • Decision-right and source map
  • Stakeholder listening across markets and functions
Days 31-60

Instrument the portfolio

Create one practical executive truth.

  • Priority Trace Contract
  • Decision and constraint registry
  • Escalation thresholds
  • Baseline operating measures
Days 61-90

Run the cadence

Pilot on real strategic work.

  • Two or three priority pilots
  • Pre-read and decision review
  • Obstacle removal and owner coaching
  • Retire low-value reporting
Days 91-120

Scale the mechanism

Institutionalize without centralizing everything.

  • Quarterly strategy rhythm
  • Cascading functional cadences
  • Learning close and playbook
  • CEO force-multiplier handoff

Executive questions

The questions I would use to discover the real mandate.

01Which three decisions consume disproportionate CEO and ELT time today?

03Where do market-specific requirements create real value - and where do they create avoidable variation?
04What must not break while Metrc modernizes the platform and product model?
05Where does internal misalignment show up first for regulators, licensees, partners, or support teams?
06What would make this role indispensable in six months - and unnecessary for routine coordination?
