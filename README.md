# Metrc Chief of Staff Candidate Vision

Independent candidate campaign for Russell Dudek, built for the Metrc Chief of Staff role supplied directly in chat. No verified public job-posting URL was available at build time.

## Candidate thesis

**Give every strategic priority a traceable path to outcome.**

The campaign presents an Executive Priority Trace that connects outcome, accountable custody, dependencies, decision thresholds, evidence, and closure across CEO-level priorities.

## Routes
- `index.html` - candidate vision
- `resume.html` - exactly two printable pages
- `cover-letter.html` - exactly one printable page
- `interview-brief.html` - four-page interview thesis brief
- `120-day-plan.html` - four-page entry plan
- `priority-trace-review.html` - two-page operating artifact

## Generated PDFs
All print-oriented routes provide direct PDF downloads under `docs/`.

## Brand assets
Official Metrc logo assets and provenance are documented in `brand-intelligence.md` and stored under `assets/brand/`. The campaign is clearly labeled as independent work and does not imply Metrc endorsement.

## Public-source grounding
The analysis uses current official Metrc company, leadership, product, API, Retail ID, and 2025-2026 newsroom sources. Company-specific interpretations are presented as hypotheses for discovery rather than undisclosed internal facts.

Designed for GitHub Pages from `main` and `/ (root)`.
