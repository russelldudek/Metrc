Independent candidate entry plan
Entry principle

Earn the right to simplify.

A new Chief of Staff should not begin by imposing meetings, dashboards, or templates. The first responsibility is to understand the existing operating system, the trust network around the CEO, and the few places where greater traceability would materially improve decisions and outcomes.

120-day objective

Build a trusted, decision-focused operating mechanism that increases CEO leverage, strengthens executive ownership, and improves progress on the company's highest-value cross-functional priorities.

Operating guardrails

Do

- Use existing information before creating new reporting
- Preserve functional ownership and expert authority
- Focus executive forums on decisions and exceptions
- Label hypotheses and test them through discovery
- Retire mechanisms that do not improve outcomes

Do not

- Become the default owner of every cross-functional task
- Create a shadow project-management organization
- Confuse activity visibility with business progress
- Escalate issues that can be resolved locally
- turn confidentiality into opacity or status theater

Pilot selection criteria

Strategic valueMaterial to company direction
Cross-functional loadMultiple real dependencies
Decision frictionMeaningful unresolved tradeoff

Potential public-signal categories include platform modernization, a market implementation, Retail ID expansion, or a strategic partnership. Actual pilots would be selected with the CEO and ELT based on confidential priorities.

Pilot operating steps

StepActionGuardrail

61-90Tighten the CEO and ELT operating rhythm based on evidence from the pilots.

Cadence changes to test

- Shift weekly reviews toward exceptions, decisions, and ownership.
- Consolidate duplicate status reporting into one portfolio trace.
- Introduce pre-read standards proportional to the decision.
- Clarify which forum owns which class of decision.
- Establish decision receipts and explicit follow-through.
- Retire meetings or reports that do not change action.

Day-90 deliverables

Two validated priority traces, CEO / ELT cadence charter, strategic portfolio view, decision-threshold taxonomy, reporting-retirement list, and a draft year-one Chief of Staff scorecard.

Year-one operating roadmap

Quarter 2
Extend trace discipline to the strategic portfolio; strengthen executive review quality; establish board-facing synthesis where appropriate.

Quarter 3
Use cross-market, product, and customer learning to improve prioritization and operating consistency.

Quarter 4
Review the Chief of Staff mechanism itself: what improved decisions, what became overhead, and what should be transferred, automated, or stopped.

Year-end
Present evidence of focus, decision velocity, ownership, trust, adoption, and learning - not a list of meetings managed.

Day-120 outcome

A trusted Chief of Staff operating model that is useful enough to be adopted, light enough to sustain, explicit enough to improve accountability, and humble enough to be changed when the evidence says it should.
