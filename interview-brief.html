Executive thesis

Give every strategic priority a traceable path to outcome.

Metrc's Chief of Staff opportunity is to turn a growing portfolio of company priorities into one decision-ready operating trace: outcome, owner, dependencies, threshold, evidence, and closure.

The actual mandate

Increase the CEO's leverage and the Executive Leadership Team's execution velocity by making strategic priorities easier to understand, decide, own, and complete - without centralizing functional ownership or creating a shadow operating system.

Executive Priority Trace
Role-specific operating model
Russell Dudek

Not another project dashboard

The trace is a lightweight executive decision record. It lives above functional project plans and below strategy decks, preserving ownership while making cross-functional movement visible.

The six fields

01 Outcome tag
What must become true?
State the business, customer, regulatory, or public-value outcome in observable terms.
02 Accountable custody
Who owns movement?
Name the executive owner, operating owner, and boundaries that remain with each function.
03 Dependency map
What conditions must connect?
Expose product, engineering, market, customer, partner, legal, data, adoption, and capacity dependencies.
04 Decision threshold
What requires executive judgment?
Define local authority, escalation triggers, stop criteria, and the actual decision maker.
05 Evidence receipt
What proves movement?
Use a balanced set of outcome, reliability, adoption, economics, trust, and learning signals.
06 Closure / learning
What should compound?
Record the decision, result, residual risk, and reusable learning; retire the record when complete.

Five executive questions

  1. Which two or three priorities most need the CEO's attention to move - and which currently consume attention without changing outcomes?
  2. Where does Metrc most often lose decision velocity: incomplete information, unclear ownership, cross-functional dependencies, or risk tolerance?
  3. How should the Chief of Staff divide work with the executive team so the role increases accountability rather than centralizing it?
  4. What would make platform modernization, market implementations, and product expansion feel like one coherent company strategy rather than parallel programs?
  5. At the end of year one, what evidence would convince the CEO and board that this role materially improved execution?

Closing thesis

Metrc's product promise is traceability. The Chief of Staff can make that promise operational inside the company by ensuring every strategic priority has a recognizable outcome, accountable custody, visible dependencies, a decision threshold, evidence of progress, and clean closure.
